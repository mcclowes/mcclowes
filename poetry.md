# Poetry

Blackout poems drawn from Batool Abu Akleen's *48 Kg* (trans. from the Arabic).
Each piece is made by erasure — the words that remain are hers; the shape is mine.

---

## Day eleven

Weeping was my lot.
Half asleep, two little sparrows left their own land
'refugee'
I kiss their bodies,
the colour of wheat in summer,
they complain into the watering.
It was my lot to be the cloud!
